The thesis

One number that says: good player, cheap market.

Every player is scored inside their own position group, so a defender is measured against defenders and a goalkeeper against goalkeepers. The Undervalued Score is deliberately simple — and every point is auditable back to the underlying stats.

The differentiator

We audit the data before you act on it.

Borrowed straight from RevOps: before anyone works a pipeline, you scan it. ScoutEdge grades the whole database on completeness — missing values, unknown representation, missing contacts, stale records and duplicates — so you know exactly how much to trust a shortlist before you pick up the phone.

Filter the board and the scan re-grades in real time. It's the difference between a list of names and a pipeline you can defend.

The workspace

A scouting terminal you arrange your way.

Everything a lower-tier scouting desk needs, in one board you can rebuild panel by panel.

Customizable dashboard

Add, remove and drag every panel and KPI — filters, weights, insights, the table — into the layout that fits how you scout. It's saved automatically.

RevOps insights

A live data-health scan, a pipeline funnel from watchlist to hottest target, and a routing engine that tells you exactly who to contact for each player.

Transparent scoring

Slide the weights for goals, progression, defence and youth. The Undervalued Score, percentiles and system-fit all recompute in front of you.

Global coverage

5,000 players across 38 countries on six continents — with a dedicated goalkeeper model, not a striker's metrics forced onto a keeper.

Shortlists & notes

Star players into a shortlist and write private notes that sync to your account across devices — the start of a scouting record only you own.

Export & compare

Compare up to four players on a radar chart, then export any view or your shortlist to CSV to drop into the report your director actually reads.
